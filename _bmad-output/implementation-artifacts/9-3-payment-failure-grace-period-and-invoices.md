## Story

As an **owner**,
I want **clear handling of payment failures with a 7-day grace period (with warning emails and automatic retries, a persistent red strip while I'm at risk, and a graceful day-7 auto-downgrade to Free that never deletes any of my data), plus a full invoice history I can filter, export to CSV, download as PDF, and email to my accountant**,
so that **I have time and clear signposting to resolve a billing problem before losing paid features, I never lose my center's content even if I lapse, and I can manage my financial records for accounting.**

This is the **grace + invoices layer of Epic 9's money half**, built ON TOP of 9-2a's already-shipped, signature-verified, idempotent Polar receiver and `invoices` table. It does **NOT** rebuild the receiver, the dedup, the signature verifier, the `invoices` write path, or the downgrade-apply machinery — those shipped in 9-2a (`rescopes: [9.3]`). It layers: the `payment_failed`/`past_due`/recovery dispatch cases; the MockClock-driven 7-day grace state machine (days 0/3/5/6/7) on the existing jobs queue; the day-7 auto-downgrade-to-Free that asserts **zero row deletion** (R24); the recovery path; the owner-only app-wide red grace strip (s73); and the invoice-history read/export UI (s70).

## Correct-Course Amendments (v0.2 — 2026-10-06, Ducdo R1–R4 after party-mode review)

The elapsed-day index for the email days stays `floor((Now − grace_period_start)/24h)`, but the day-7 ExpireGraceToFree tick is scheduled at the 23:59 boundary and must fire AT it and **not before** (an owner who pays at day-7 10:00 is still inside the window). Reconcile: D5 scheduling, AC5/AC6 wording, the BE red's day-7 assertion, the FE `graceEndsAt` fixture (already 23:59). *Supersedes any midnight interpretation.*

## Acceptance Criteria

### A. Payment-failure → grace entry (D2/D6 — FR-65)

1. **Given** the shipped `ProcessPolarEvent` dispatcher, **When** Polar delivers a payment-failure event (verified name — `subscription.past_due`/`order.payment_failed`/`invoice.payment_failed`, cited in Dev Agent Record) for a center with an active paid subscription, **Then** in the inherited one-tx-dedup-dispatch path the handler sets `subscriptions.status='past_due'`, records `grace_period_start = clk.Now()` + `payment_failed_at`, and enqueues the first `billing_grace_tick` job (D5) — all tenant-scoped (`SET LOCAL`, SEC-6) under the confused-deputy tenant resolution INHERITED from 9-2a. No raw-body tenant trust (R11 unchanged).
2. **Given** a duplicate/re-delivered payment-failure event (same `webhook-id`, OR a second failure event while already `past_due`), **Then** it is idempotent: the `polar_webhook_events` PK dedup drops an exact re-delivery, AND an already-`past_due` center does NOT restart the grace clock, re-stamp `grace_period_start`, or double-enqueue ticks (a target-state check, not event-id alone — the 9-2a D19 idiom). A test asserts the clock/tick count is unchanged on the second delivery.

### B. Grace state machine — MockClock days 0/3/5/6/7 (D2/D5 — FR-65, R21)

3. **Given** the `billing_grace_tick` JobType on the existing jobs queue (`next_attempt_at`, MockClock-driven; the worker imports `BillingService` — GFW-7), **When** the grace clock advances, **Then** warning emails are sent on **day 0, day 3, day 5, and day 6** (new `RenderPaymentFailed*` templates via the existing Resend sender; each email names the reason + the day-7 deadline + a link to payment settings), and the NEXT tick is enqueued with the correct future `next_attempt_at` until day 7.
4. **Given** days **3 and 5**, **Then** the tick requests Polar to re-collect the charge (D2 — a thin `internal/polar` retry call if exposed; else our tick drives only the emails and Polar's native retry reports back via the webhook — VERIFY + cite). **We never collect payment ourselves.** `grace_retry_count` increments per retry attempt.
5. **Given** the entire 7-day flow is driven by `MockClock` (never `time.Sleep`, TEST-BE-5), **Then** an integration suite advances the clock through days 0 / 3 / 5 / 6 / 7-23:59 and asserts, explicitly per boundary: the right emails sent, the day-3/day-5 retries requested, and the day-7 auto-downgrade fired (C6). A tick that re-runs for an already-processed day is a no-op (idempotent — D5).

### C. Day-7 auto-downgrade — zero data loss (D3 — FR-65, R24)

6. **Given** day-7 (23:59) with payment NOT recovered, **When** the final grace tick fires, **Then** the center is auto-downgraded to **Free**: `subscriptions.status='cancelled'`, `plan='free'` (epic:179) applied via the 9-2a zero-deletion downgrade-apply path (re-points `centers.storage_limit_bytes` to the Free ceiling; captures the `center_resource_baselines` high-water so re-upgrade restores — reuse `CaptureResourceBaselines`). AI credits freeze (D25 tier-gate — no new code), storage uploads block (4.4a ceiling), new-resource/seat adds block (9-1a `Check*`). The grace cols are cleared; pending ticks cancelled.
7. **Given** the auto-downgrade (R24, the hard invariant), **Then** an integration test (MockClock → day 7) asserts **ZERO rows deleted** across `classes, exercises, submissions, enrollments, knowledge_files, ai_credit_ledger` (+ `subscriptions`/`ai_credits`/`invoices` rows survive) via a **PK-set snapshot per table** (not `COUNT` — delete+reinsert nets zero). Re-upgrade (a later `subscription.active`) restores full access. Only state flags / plan / limits change. (Epic:185-188 verbatim mitigation.)
8. **Given** the deferred heavy read-only surface (D3 → FU-9-3-READONLY), **Then** 9.3 does NOT add per-endpoint mutate-guards for existing over-cap content; the shipped block-for-new + AI/storage freeze are the pause semantics, and the gap is documented in `deferred-work.md`. (No AC asserts "existing class rejects a write" — that is the FU.)

### D. Recovery (D6 — FR-65)

9. **Given** a center `past_due` within the 7-day window, **When** payment is recovered (Polar fires `order.paid`/`subscription.active` before day 7 — treated as recovery because the center is `past_due`), **Then** grace is cleared (`status='active'`, grace cols nulled), ALL pending `billing_grace_tick` jobs are cancelled (no further emails/retries/downgrade), and the plan is restored — in the inherited idempotent one-tx dispatch. The red strip (E) clears immediately on the next summary fetch.
