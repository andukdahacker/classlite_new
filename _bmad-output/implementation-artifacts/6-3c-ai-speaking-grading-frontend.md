## Story

As a **Teacher**,
I want **to trigger AI grading on a Speaking submission and review its auto-transcript, proposed band scores (with rationale + confidence), and timestamped moments in the s24 grading view — accepting, editing, or dismissing each into my grading draft, with AI and my own notes shown together chronologically on the timeline**,
so that **I evaluate spoken responses in a fraction of the time while remaining the one who commits the final grade.**

**Scope:** The frontend AI-review layer over the **shipped 6-3a s24 speaking grading view** (waveform player, "Pin here" comments, 2×2 speaking band inputs, timeline `NotesRail`, durable `useSpeakingGradingDraft`) and the **shipped 6-3b backend** (skill-branched enqueue + `ai_grade_speaking` worker + `AISpeakingGradeResult` + `aiSpeakingSuggestion` read + auto-refund). New: a `useAiGradeSpeakingJob` enqueue+poll hook (twin of the shipped 6-2b `useAiGradeJob`, narrowing on `moments`); an `AiSpeakingGradePanel` control (Run + confirm + generating/slow/failed states); an `AISpeakingGradeSuggestion` domain surface (four-criterion band strip + the per-moment `AiMomentCard`); **AI moment proposals interleaved into the shipped `NotesRail` timeline** (SD5) with **AI markers on the waveform** (a `source` widening of `WaveformPin`, SD6); a greenfield **transcript panel** with the `partial_success` value-first state (SD7); merge-into-draft via the already-reserved `SpeakingDraftComment.source:'ai'` (SD2); a −1-credit confirm gate on every enqueue (SD4); the >30s/>60s slow messaging + non-blocking "ready — Review?" overlay + `audio_unavailable` refund toast + invalid-scores empty-form state (SD8).

**Explicitly NOT in this story:** any backend (6-3b shipped the endpoint, worker, `AISpeakingGradeResult`, refund, and the `aiSpeakingSuggestion` read — and 6-3b0 shipped the transcoder); the grade write (6-3a `POST …/grade` speaking body, already consumed by `useGradeSpeaking`); the additive `transcriptRef` grade field (deferred, O2); Writing AI grading (6-2b, shipped); the first-run card (writing-only, SD9); the 402 balance gate + credit-history UI (6.5). See **Out of Scope**.

## Acceptance Criteria

### A. Enqueue + poll + credit gate

1. **Run + confirm (epic AC L187-188; SD4).** In the s24 view a **"Run AI grading"** control (sentence case) triggers the shipped `AiGradeConfirmDialog` showing the **−1 credit** cost. On confirm, `useAiGradeSpeakingJob.enqueue()` POSTs `/api/submissions/{submissionId}/ai-grade` (empty body; the server skill-branches to `ai_grade_speaking` from the DB exercise — SEC-7, never a client `type`) → the panel enters a *generating* state and polls `GET /api/jobs/{jobId}` with **2s → 4s → 8s** backoff. **No auto-enqueue on mount/remount** — idle until the teacher confirms.
2. **Idempotent 200 (SD1; 6-3b D6).** A `200` enqueue response (an `ai_grade_speaking` job is already pending/processing for this submission) is handled identically to `202` — the returned `{ jobId }` is polled; the UI does not imply a second credit was spent.
3. **Re-run gate (SD4).** When a completed `aiSpeakingSuggestion` already exists, "Run AI grading" opens the confirm dialog with an explicit re-run + re-charge warning; a fresh job enqueues only on confirm. Reopening/remounting the view with an existing suggestion NEVER re-enqueues.
4. **Enqueue-time rejections surface cleanly (6-3b D12).** A `409 SUBMISSION_TOO_LONG` (recording exceeds `maxAIGradeAudioDurationMs`, rejected **before** the credit deduct) or `409 SUBMISSION_NOT_GRADABLE` returns the panel to idle with an inline, i18n'd explanation — **no credit was spent, no poll starts, no false refund toast.**

### B. Suggestion review — band strip + interleaved moment timeline

5. **Band-strip proposals (epic AC L189-191).** On `complete`, the AI band strip renders the four **Speaking** criterion proposals — `fluencyCoherence`, `lexicalResource`, `grammaticalRange`, `pronunciation`, each `{ band, rationale, confidence }` — with a per-criterion **Accept / Edit / Dismiss**, the transcription-meta line (SD7), and the disclaimer **"Suggestion — teacher always decides the final band."**. A preview of the AI overall band reuses the exact treatment `SpeakingBandInputs` already renders (do NOT introduce a new typography token). Accept writes `draft.scores[criterion]`; Edit lets the teacher change the band before applying; Dismiss drops the proposal.
6. **Interleaved AI moment cards (epic AC L197-198; SD5).** AI moments render as **"AI draft" cards inline in the shipped `NotesRail`**, sorted with the teacher's own notes by `timestampMs`, each with: AI avatar (vs the teacher's own), type tag (Error red / Praise green / Suggestion amber via the shared `toCardType` `'suggestion'→'suggest'` map), criterion tag, **teacher-only confidence badge** (High/Medium), and **Accept / Edit / Dismiss**. Accept appends a `SpeakingDraftComment{ source:'ai' }`; Edit opens the text/criterion for change before applying; Dismiss drops it. A `timestampMs===null` moment renders in the rail's **general (unpinned) zone**, never dropped.
7. **AI markers on the waveform (SD6; epic AC L191-192).** Each timestamped AI moment (accepted or still a proposal) shows a marker on the waveform timeline, visually distinct from teacher pins, participating in the existing cluster/active-highlight behavior. Clicking an AI marker seeks + highlights its rail card (bidirectional, reusing the shipped pin↔card wiring). Null-timestamp moments get no marker.
8. **Accept all praise (epic AC L197 parity with 6-2b).** A bulk **"Accept all praise"** action accepts every praise-type AI moment into the draft in one action; other types untouched; a praise card currently open in Edit is **skipped** (its in-progress buffer is not silently discarded — 6-2b patch). Accepted suggestions populate the draft but the teacher can still modify before Submit — **nothing auto-applies on load.**
9. **Confidence + rationale are teacher-only (UX-DR22; 6-3b D-teacher-only).** Confidence badges and rationale appear only in the teacher s24 view. On accept they are DROPPED — `buildSpeakingGradeInput()` sends neither (wire `TimestampedComment`/`criterionScores` unchanged). **Negative test:** the student result speaking-playback path (`ResultSpeakingPlayback`, 5-5a) renders NO confidence/rationale/transcript/AI-chip for an accepted moment (it reads as the teacher's own note).

### C. Transcript panel + partial success

10. **View transcript (epic AC L193-196; SD7).** On `complete` with `transcriptionStatus==='available'`, a **"View transcript"** control reveals the full `transcript` (teacher-only, collapsible) plus the transcription meta (audio duration from `analyzedDurationMs`, analysis time from `latencyMs`).
11. **Partial success is value-first (epic AC L207-217; SD7 / D15).** On `complete` with `transcriptionStatus==='unavailable'` the panel leads with **"Transcript unavailable — band proposals below"** (a success state, **NOT** a refund/apology); the four bands + moments render and are fully reviewable. The credit is NOT refunded and the UI must not suggest it was.

### D. Rehydrate + non-triggering reader

12. **Reopen rehydrate (SD3; 6-3b hand-off).** Reopening the s24 view (no active job) with `view.aiSpeakingSuggestion !== null` renders the latest suggestion set as reviewable — the teacher resumes Accept/Edit/Dismiss; the accepted-band set is seeded from `draft.scores` so an already-applied-then-manually-edited band is not re-offered as a plain Accept (6-2b patch). Moments already merged into a persisted draft are not duplicated.
13. **Non-triggering teacher (SD3).** A co-teacher/admin who did not trigger the run sees the suggestion via `view.aiSpeakingSuggestion` (class-shared), NEVER via the creator-private poll. The UI must not attempt to poll a job it did not create.

### E. Slow + failure paths

14. **Slow messaging (epic AC parity with 6-2b; L141-143 pattern).** While polling: after **30s** show "Taking longer than expected — AI is still working"; after **60s** show "This is unusually slow — you can start grading manually and AI suggestions will appear when ready". (Elapsed-since-enqueue, distinct from the 5-min hard `stuck`.) No intermediate `retrying` status is surfaced (SD8).
15. **Non-blocking ready overlay (epic AC L145-147 pattern; SD3).** If the teacher has started manual grading (session-edited flag, not a seeded/revise draft — 6-2b patch) while the job is still processing, result arrival shows a **non-blocking** "AI suggestions are ready — Review?" overlay (`aria-live`) with a merge action — it does NOT auto-open or clobber in-progress work.
16. **Audio-unavailable refund + re-record (epic AC L211-217; SD8).** On terminal `errorDetails==='audio_unavailable'` show the refund toast **"We couldn't process this recording — your AI credit has been returned. Try again."** and an inline failed-state with **"Ask student to re-record"**. Fired once per episode; not re-fired on a language switch.
17. **Invalid-scores / invalid-output states (epic AC L133-135 pattern).** On terminal `errorDetails==='invalid_band_scores'` show "AI produced invalid scores — please grade manually" with **all form fields empty** (never pre-filled with bad data). On `invalid_ai_response` (malformed/incomplete) show the "AI returned invalid output. Credit returned. Please grade manually." refund toast. A 3× poll infra failure shows the inline retry **without** a credit-returned toast (`poll_error`, 6-2b patch).
18. **Loading/Empty/Error trilogy (UX-1).** The AI panel uses skeletons mirroring the final layout (no centered spinner), an inline retry on poll error (not a full-page error), and all copy via i18n (no hardcoded English, no HTTP codes).

### F. i18n + a11y

19. **i18n parity (UX-2 / TEST-FE-4).** Every new string is a `speakingGrading.ai.*` key added to BOTH `en.json` AND `vi.json` in the same change (reuse `criterion.*` for the four criterion labels; do not re-key them). A `STORY_6_3C` closed-literal key list + prefix-ratchet parity test asserts both locales, mirroring `story-6-3a-i18n.test.ts` / `story-6-2b-i18n.test.ts`.
20. **Accessibility (TEST-FE-5 / UX-DR16).** The AI panel + confirm dialog + transcript disclosure pass axe; Accept/Edit/Dismiss are reachable by role/label; the ready overlay uses `aria-live`; focus returns to the trigger on dialog close; the waveform AI markers are keyboard-reachable per the existing 6-3a marker model.
