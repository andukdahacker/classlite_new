# Gold-set inter-rater overlap — checking the ruler (Story 11-0b, AC9/DD9)

_You cannot trust a grader against a ruler nobody checked. A tight tolerance against a crooked ruler is precision in service of a lie (John)._

## Why this exists (DD9 — the #1 convergent party-mode finding)

The gold set has two halves that never overlap by default:

- **Published Cambridge/IDP anchors** — examiner-banded, but they skew to **bands 7–8** (model answers) and are plausibly in the model's training data (interim sanity check only).
- **Center-banded bands 4–6 cohort** — banded by the **center teacher** (NOT a certified examiner), and this is where real students actually sit and where ±0.5 is commercially load-bearing (the authoritative cohort).

Because the two halves cover **different band ranges**, they are never banded on the same scripts, so the "certified examiner = escalation on disagreement" safety net rarely fires. Before the gold set can bless the grader, **its own bands-4-6 banding must be inter-rater-checked.**

## The required overlap zone

Take a **handful (≥5 recommended)** of the bands-4-6 scripts and have them banded by **BOTH**:

1. the **center teacher** (who bands the rest of the 4–6 cohort), and
2. a **certified IELTS examiner**.

Report the **inter-rater agreement** on these overlap scripts (per-criterion and overall).

## Agreement threshold + escalation rule

- **Disagreement threshold:** overall |Δ| ≤ **0.5** AND every per-criterion |Δ| ≤ **1.0** on each overlap script (mirrors the human inter-rater reality the calibration tolerances assume).
- **If exceeded on any overlap script:** the **examiner's bands are authoritative**, and the center teacher's bands **on the whole 4–6 cohort are re-reviewed** before the set is admitted as evidence. The grader is never calibrated against an unchecked ruler.
- Record the agreement summary (mean |Δ|, worst script) and the admit/re-review decision here, and reference it from the calibration report.

## Admit decision

- [ ] Overlap zone banded by both raters, agreement within threshold → **gold set admitted** as evidence for the authoritative calibration run.
- [ ] Threshold exceeded → examiner bands adopted, cohort re-reviewed, overlap re-run → then admit.
